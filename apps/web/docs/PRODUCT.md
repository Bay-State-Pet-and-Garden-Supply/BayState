# Bay State Pet & Garden Supply

**Register:** brand (storefront) and product (admin panel)

## Users
- **Storefront:** Local customers seeking high-quality pet food, farm supplies, and gardening equipment. They value reliability, heritage, and expertise.
- **Admin:** Store staff and inventory managers needing high data density, clarity, and speed.

## Brand & Tone
- **Identity:** Heritage & Trust. Traditional, reliable, established.
- **Voice:** Knowledgeable, warm, professional, grounded.
- **Vibe:** A classic hardware and pet supply store with oak shelves, well-lit and organized.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Trust over Flash:** Prioritize legibility and clear hierarchy over trendy UI patterns.
2. **Warmth:** Use warm neutrals and deep forest greens; avoid stark #000 or #fff.
3. **Register Alignment:** Storefront uses serif headings for brand expression; Admin panel uses dense sans-serif for utility, but shares the same color palette.

## Anti-References
- **No SaaS-y Slop:** Avoid generic soft glassmorphism, floating glowing blobs, or identical card grids.
- **No Brutalism:** Remove heavy black borders (`border-4 border-zinc-900`) and blocky, offset solid shadows.
